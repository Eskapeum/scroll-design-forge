# Build fingerprints

This workspace's record of completed scroll-design-forge work supports useful
comparison and intentional reuse. It is not a novelty quota or a list of
patterns that have become unavailable.

The current procedure is in the skill's `references/uniqueness.md`. Compare
relevant same-brand versions, campaign siblings, or unrelated recent work.
Separate brand fit from distinctiveness. Similarity can be desirable.

## Comparison notes

For relevant comparisons, record:

- Comparator and why it is relevant.
- Observable overlap and differences in the rendered work.
- What the brief asks to preserve.
- Whether the overlap is intentional or worth revisiting.
- The reasoned decision; do not count correlated fields as independent points.

There is no four-of-six gate. Grammar, nav, hero, and close often reflect the
same organizing decision. No grammar, page length, palette, or signature is
reserved by use.

## Recording a completed build

Append a factual, versioned row when the build is completed and this registry
is in scope. Link its actual artifact or verification evidence and build
identity; a port number alone does not identify a build.

Preserve historical rows, including superseded versions. Correct factual
errors transparently rather than rewriting history to make a comparison pass.
Record intentional reuse explicitly. A review-only request does not update
the registry.

Historical examples in the skill's `references/examples.md` are optional
illustration and do not constrain this workspace.
